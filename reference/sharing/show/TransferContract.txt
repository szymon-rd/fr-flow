+ transferContract  contract on transfer  accepted
  The owner of a document, while they may edit it, hands it on to someone who may edit it: that person is then its owner, with an edit share of it for good in place of any share of it they had. When anyone else asks, or the new owner may not edit it, nothing changes.
  why: Q14: only the owner transfers a document, to someone who may edit it; the new owner's share makes sure they always may edit it.

Formally (as written in Scala)
  post (every call of transfer):
    forall((lib: Term[Library], t: Term[BigInt], by: Term[PersonId], d: Term[DocumentId], to: Term[PersonId]) =>
        transfer(lib, t, by, d, to) === (owns(lib, by, d) && mayEdit(lib, t, by, Target[Target.Doc](d)) && mayEdit(lib, t, to, Target[Target.Doc](d))).fold(
          lib.copy((documents = lib.documents.map(doc => (doc.id === d).fold(doc.copy((owner = to)), doc)),
            shares = Share(Target[Target.Doc](d), Grantee[Grantee.Person](to), Level.Edit, none[BigInt]) ::
              sharesWithout(lib.shares, Target[Target.Doc](d), Grantee[Grantee.Person](to)))), lib))

An owner's share is with them in person, not through a team, so leaving a team never takes their documents from them."

  uses       - cover (definition): "A share of a document covers that document; a share of a folder covers the folder, every folder inside it, and every document inside any of them."
