- inTrash  definition  accepted
  A document is in the trash when it was put there and not restored since.
  why: Q8: a document in the trash gives nobody access, whatever its shares say; restoring it brings them back.

Formally (as written in Scala)
    inTrash := (lib: Term[Library], d: Term[DocumentId]) =>
    lib.documents.exists(doc => doc.id === d && doc.trashed.isDefined)

  used by    + accessContract (contract): "A person's access to a document not in the trash is worked out from their teams and the document's folders: among the shares in force with them or one of their teams, of the document or one of the folders around it, it is edit if one is an edit share, view if there is any, and none otherwise. A document in the trash gives no access."
