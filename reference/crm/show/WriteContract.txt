+ writeContract  contract on write  accepted
  A write keeps every record, leaves the records of other companies as they were, does what its author asked of their company's records, and changes nothing else.
  why: The writing rules, stated for one write on a store: what the user asked takes effect, no record is lost, and nothing else changes, of their company or another's. A write the author's company may not make changes nothing.

Formally (as written in Scala)
  post (every call of write):
    forall((s: Term[CrmStore], w: Term[WriteCmd]) => s.records.forall(r => write(s, w).records.contains(r)) &&
      write(s, w).records.filter(r => r.tenant =!= w.by.tenant) === s.records.filter(r => r.tenant =!= w.by.tenant) && takesEffect(s, w, write(s, w)) &&
      onlyAsked(s, w, write(s, w)))
  check:
    (s, w, t) => takesEffect(s, w, t) && t.records.filter(r => r.tenant =!= w.by.tenant) === s.records.filter(r => r.tenant =!= w.by.tenant)
  exempt from noCrossTenantRead: the store a write returns goes to the write side, never to a reader: reads go through readContract
