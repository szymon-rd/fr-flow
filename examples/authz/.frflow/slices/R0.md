### R0 NoUnauthorizedAccess, requirements (this layer)

**Statement.** Every recorded access was permitted by the policy in force when it was decided.

**Rationale.** States the security goal without any mechanism. Policies change over time, so the goal is relative to the policy version in force at decision time; published versions never change.

**Requirement `Safe`.** Every logged access was permitted by the policy version it was decided under, and that version is not in the future.
