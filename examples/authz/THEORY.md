## Vocabulary

- **User**: A principal: a person or a service that can act on the system.
- **Resource**: Anything access to which is controlled.
- **Action**: An operation a principal can perform on a resource.
- **Token** (`Nat`): Identifier of a bearer credential issued at login.

## Assumptions

Guarantees hold for every environment that satisfies these assumptions.

- **A1 PresenterIsSessionPrincipal.** A request presenting a token bound to principal u is issued by u.
  *Justification:* Tokens are issued only to authenticated principals and are unforgeable and not shared. To be discharged by the authentication refinement (T2a) under the unforgeability of signatures.

### T1 AuthnThenAuthz

Refines **T0**. Status: unapproved, proven.

Access is granted only to the principal bound to the presented token, under the current policy.

*Rationale:* Separates identity (sessions created at login) from permission (policy decision). The system cannot observe who sends a request, only which token is presented, so the link between the token and the real requester is an explicit environment assumption (A1).
